# Movement Notes

## Phase 1: Micro-Movement

We are adding small player movement inside the cabin.

Not full walking.

---

## Why

- increases immersion
- improves interaction feel
- gives physical presence

---

## What we avoid

- full FPS movement
- collision system
- complex player controller

---

## Constraints

- small movement radius
- still driver-focused
- camera remains primary

---

## Future

- seat switching
- proximity-based interaction
- possible full walking later
